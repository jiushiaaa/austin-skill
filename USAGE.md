# 奥斯汀 / J. L. Austin — 使用 / Usage

## 中文

安装后直接提出问题，说明事实、目标和不确定之处。

- 默认：`用奥斯汀的思想分析这个问题。保持中性，区分原作与推断。`
- 可选表达：`明确开启奥斯汀人物表达模式，讨论这个问题。注明这是模拟。`
- 退出：`退出人物表达模式，恢复中性分析。`
- 文游：`审读这个剧情中的哲学冲突，只取思想，不采用作者口吻。`
- 比较：分别调用各人物模块，列出各自前提、依据和分歧，不强行综合。

默认中性思想模式；只有明确开启时才读取表达附录。进入文游创作或审读会恢复中性。任何模式都不冒充本人、不编引语、不把现代推演说成作者历史立场。研究材料只覆盖注明的实际读取范围；需要精确原句时仍须核对原作。

## English

After installation, describe your question, the relevant facts, and what remains uncertain.

- Default: `Analyze this question using J. L. Austin's ideas. Stay neutral and distinguish source claims from inference.`
- Optional expression: `Explicitly enable the J. L. Austin expression mode for this question. Identify it as a simulation.`
- Exit: `Leave expression mode and return to neutral analysis.`
- Fiction: `Review the philosophical conflict in this scene. Use the ideas without adopting the author's voice.`
- Comparison: invoke each thinker separately, identifying premises, evidence, and disagreements without forcing a synthesis.

Neutral analysis is the default. Read the expression appendix only after an explicit request. Fiction writing or review returns to neutral analysis. Neither mode impersonates the historical person, invents quotations, or presents modern extrapolations as historical positions. Coverage is limited to the reading ranges documented in the research notes; verify exact quotations against their sources.

Research notes remain in their original languages. You may ask the runtime for an English explanation; such generated explanations are not independently reviewed translations of the entire source corpus.
